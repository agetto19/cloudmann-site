Scooter Blitz Privacy Policy

Effective 19 October 2026

Scooter Blitz ("the app") is an iPhone racing game made by Cloudmann Consulting ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles, who handles it, and the choices you have. In short: we don't run any servers that collect your personal information, and we don't have user accounts. Your game progress stays on your device. The only third parties involved are Apple, for Game Center and purchases, and Google, for optional rewarded ads.

1. Information stored on your device

Your progress, currencies (Coins and Volts), rides, upgrades, settings and race history are stored only on your iPhone. A copy of your purchased balance is kept in your device's Keychain so it survives a reinstall; it is not synced to iCloud and is not sent to us. We cannot see this data. Deleting the app removes it, except the Keychain copy, which iOS keeps on the device.

2. Apple services

  • Game Center. Online races, ghost races, tournaments and leaderboards use Apple's Game Center. If you sign in to Game Center, Apple shares your Game Center player identifier and display name with the game so other players can see you in races and on leaderboards. Scores, race times and ghost replays you choose to send are shared through Game Center. Apple's handling of this data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy. We do not copy it to our own servers.
  • In-app purchases. Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment details. Apple tells the app only which item was bought, so it can be delivered and restored.
  • Track downloads. Some tracks download after you install the app. Your device requests these files through Apple's download services, and the requests include standard technical information, such as your IP address, that is needed to deliver the file. We don't use it for anything else.

3. Rewarded ads (Google AdMob)

The app offers optional rewarded ads: you can choose to watch an ad in exchange for a free in-game perk. There are no banner or pop-up ads. The ad software (Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google's User Messaging Platform) starts only when you tap to watch an ad. The exception is a consent check that runs once, after your first completed race.

When an ad is shown, Google may collect the following:

DataLinked to youUsed for tracking
Device advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow trackingYesYes
Approximate location (derived from IP address)YesNo
Product interaction and advertising data (for example, ads viewed and tapped)YesNo
Crash, performance and other diagnostic dataNoNo

Google uses this data to show, measure and improve ads, and for analytics and fraud prevention, as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices. After your first race, iOS asks whether the app may track you. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", Google does not receive your advertising identifier and you get non-personalised ads with the same rewards. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. If you're in a region that requires it, Google's consent form lets you manage ad personalisation. If you buy "Remove Ads", you receive the perk without watching an ad.

4. What we don't do

  • We don't collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, precise location or health data.
  • We don't run our own analytics or crash-reporting servers.
  • We don't sell your personal information.

5. Children

Scooter Blitz is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help get it removed.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR in the EU/UK or state laws such as the CCPA in California), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to certain uses. Because we don't hold personal information about you on our own systems, most requests are best directed to Apple (Game Center and purchases) or Google (ads), using the links above. You can also contact us, and we will help.

7. Data retention and security

Game data stays on your device until you delete the app. Data handled by Apple and Google is retained under their policies. The app uses the security protections built into iOS.

8. Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, for example a new feature or service, we will update this page and change the effective date above.
