How we work

Three steps, no discovery theatre

You'll know inside one conversation whether there's something worth building. If there isn't, we'll tell you.

STEP ONE

Look at the real work

Not a workshop. We sit with the actual process — the spreadsheet, the report template, the manual review — and find where the hours go.

STEP TWO

Build the smallest useful thing

One working piece, fast, aimed at the highest-friction step. You use it before anyone scopes something bigger.

STEP THREE

Hand over the keys

Your repo, your accounts, your infrastructure, documented well enough that you could replace us.

Where projects start

A

B

C

You're curious what's possible

ValuBrief

Source-traceable valuation reports for licensed real-estate professionals.

Developed by Cloudmann Consulting

One engine. Every figure traceable.

One engine runs the CMA, the Advanced Market Brief, the BPO, appraisal support and a CRM.

The model never produces a number; the engine does the arithmetic. Weak comparables are disclosed, not buried.

Five provenance tiers

  • Sourced
  • Indicative
  • Assumption
  • You entered
  • Not found

Source-traceable analysis. Not an appraisal, not legal or tax advice.
