AI Kid privacy policy

Beta version. Last updated November 17, 2026. AI Kid is operated by Anthony Getto, Erie, Colorado, USA. Contact: anthony.getto@gmail.com.

AI Kid is a game for adults 18 and older. The kid you raise is fictional and AI-generated. This policy explains what data AI Kid handles, why, and how long it is kept.

Who processes your data

AI Kid's server runs on Oracle Cloud Infrastructure in the United States. Oracle hosts it as a service provider and doesn't use the data for its own purposes. AI generation is performed by OpenAI. iCloud backups are stored by Apple in your own iCloud account.

What stays on your device

Your kid, game progress and the photos you keep are saved on your iPhone. A device save alone does not survive deleting the app or losing the phone.

Sign in with Apple and iCloud backup

If you sign in with Apple, AI Kid receives an Apple-issued user identifier so your online requests and backups stay tied to your account. When you press Back up now, your save and kept photos are copied to your own private iCloud storage through Apple CloudKit. AI Kid's operator cannot browse your private iCloud data.

When you use AI features

Photos, work-message ideas and Talk go through AI Kid's server to OpenAI, which generates the result. A request can include your fictional kid's profile and appearance, scene and style choices, recent in-game moments, recordings you choose to send in Talk, and, for family photo modes, a photo of a consenting adult that you choose to submit. OpenAI processes these under its own terms and retention rules. AI Kid never asks for access to browse your photo library. It receives only the photo you pick. Never submit photos, video or voice of a real child.

Safety review, records and retention

  • Uploaded adult photos are screened for safety. Upload copies held for editing expire within about 10 minutes unless a review copy is created.
  • Review copies. Submitted images, including flagged uploads, and related checked results may be kept encrypted for review. They are deleted automatically 30 days after creation. Appeals do not extend that date.
  • Request and safety records (request IDs, outcomes and policy versions, but no image contents) are kept for up to 30 days.
  • Short-lived encrypted backups of review data are kept for at most 7 days and never longer than the original review copy.
  • Generated images and request history on AI Kid's server support your album and support requests.

Authorized staff may review submitted content and safety records to investigate misuse, fix problems and enforce account restrictions. An automated flag alone is not treated as proof of wrongdoing, and you can appeal a decision or restriction in Account & backup → Photo review & appeals. AI Kid does not publicly repost submitted photos.

If content appears to involve the sexual exploitation of a child, AI Kid will report it to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) as US law requires. Material connected to such a report, or to a valid legal order, is preserved for the period the law or order requires: one year for an NCMEC report under 18 U.S.C. 2258A. That can be longer than the periods above, and can continue after account deletion.

Deleting your account

In Account & backup, choose Delete AI Kid account. AI Kid revokes its Sign in with Apple access, erases your account data on AI Kid's server and on this device, and deletes your iCloud backup zone. A minimal record (a hash of the deletion receipt and any active restriction status) is kept for up to 30 days so deletions can finish safely and blocked accounts stay blocked. Copies you exported or saved elsewhere, and data already processed by OpenAI under its own retention rules, are outside this deletion. Starting over inside the game only resets the kid on your device.

What AI Kid does not do

  • No advertising, ad tracking or sale of personal data.
  • No access to your contacts, location or photo library.
  • No accounts for anyone under 18.

Your choices and rights

You can use the game without AI features, decline uploads, delete your account in the app, or email us to ask about, correct or delete your data. Depending on where you live (for example California, Colorado or the EU), you may have additional rights. We will respond to verified requests within the time the law requires.

Security

Traffic uses HTTPS. AI provider keys and review encryption keys stay on the server and are never in the app. Review copies are encrypted with per-case keys that are destroyed when the copy expires.

Changes

We will update this page when practices change and show the new date at the top.
