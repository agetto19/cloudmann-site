AI Kid support

Email anthony.getto@gmail.com. Please include your device model, iOS version, the app build (shown in TestFlight) and, for photo problems, the support reference the app shows. Do not attach the image itself.

Report a problem or harmful content

Use Account & backup → Photo review & appeals in the app to appeal a photo decision or an account restriction, or email us with the support reference. Never send images that might show a child in an unsafe or sexual way, not even to report them. Describe the issue and include the reference instead.

If a child is in immediate danger, call 911 or your local emergency number. To report suspected child sexual exploitation, you can also contact the NCMEC CyberTipline.

Delete your account

In the app, open Account & backup and choose Delete AI Kid account. If you can no longer use the app, email us from the address linked to your Apple account and we will help.
